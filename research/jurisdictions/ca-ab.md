## Business structure

- **Sole proprietorship** (recommended for a solo retailer to start). Alberta's career site describes it as "complete control and unlimited liability (meaning your personal assets are on the line for business debts)". Source: https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/self-employment/how-to-start-a-business-in-alberta/ (page updated 2025-09-23, fetched 2026-10-07)
- A business name "doesn't have a legal existence in its own right", so the sole proprietor is personally responsible for the business's debts. Source: https://www.alberta.ca/business-names (fetched 2026-10-07)
- Partnership and corporation are the alternatives; incorporation is at https://www.alberta.ca/incorporate-alberta-corporation.

## Name search and registration

- Who: an individual doing business under a name other than their own personal name must register a **trade name** under the Partnership Act. Someone trading under their own legal name does not. Source: https://www.alberta.ca/register-business-name (fetched 2026-10-07)
- How: there is no direct online filing for the public. Complete the **Declaration of Trade Name** form (https://cfr.forms.gov.ab.ca/Form/REG3018) and take it, with valid ID and payment, to an authorized **Corporate Registry service provider** (registry agent). Source: same page.
- Name search: a NUANS Business Name Report is "recommended but optional", ordered from an authorized NUANS member. Duplicate business names may exist, and a similar name can invite legal action from the existing owner. Source: same page.
- Finding an agent: https://www.alberta.ca/find-business-registry lists service providers by level. Trade names are a basic (level 1) registration.
- Fee: a government fee plus the agent's service fee. "Service fees are not regulated and may vary from one agent to another." Source: https://www.alberta.ca/find-business-registry (fetched 2026-10-07). Amounts are in the Registry agent product catalogue (https://open.alberta.ca/publications/6041328), which could not be fetched (server error). **Fee amount not confirmed.**
- An email address is mandatory when registering a business name. Source: https://www.alberta.ca/business-names.

## Federal ID

CRA Business Number through Business Registration Online, signed in with a CRA account. Shared with BC and Ontario: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/business-registration.html

## Sales tax

- Alberta charges **5% GST** and **0% provincial sales tax**; there is no provincial sales tax account to open. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html (rates effective 2025-04-01, fetched 2026-10-07)
- Must register once taxable supplies pass **$30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters**; may register voluntarily before then. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html (fetched 2026-10-07)
- Place of supply: goods shipped to other provinces are taxed at the destination's rate (see the Ontario research for the CRA source).

## Licences

No provincial general business licence. Municipal licences are local:

- **Calgary**: retail businesses need a licence, explicitly including "Online or phone sales" and "Home-based retail sales". Apply online with a myID account. Fees are in the city's business licence fee schedule. Source: https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/licences/retail.html (fetched 2026-10-07)
- **Edmonton**: home-based businesses "must have a business licence like any other business in the city". A development permit is also needed for more visible home businesses, for example with customers visiting. Fees are paid at every renewal. Source: https://www.edmonton.ca/business_economy/licences_permits/home-based-business (fetched 2026-10-07)
- Other municipalities: check locally.

## Consumer protection

Alberta's Internet Sales Contract Regulation (under the Consumer Protection Act) sets disclosure rules for online sales to consumers. Source: https://open.alberta.ca/publications/2001_081. Detailed requirements were not reviewed; step 10 only points to them.

## Ordered checklist

1. Structure and name (sole proprietorship; optional NUANS report)
2. Register a trade name at a registry agent (if not trading under own name)
3. CRA Business Number
4. GST (5%)
5. Municipal licence (Calgary and Edmonton both require one for home-based retail)
6. Insurance
7. Bank account
8. Bookkeeping and income tax
9. Payment processor
10. Legal pages and privacy
11. Product compliance
12. Supplier account
