## Pages you need

- **Privacy policy**
- **Terms of sale**
- **Shipping and returns policy**

## Privacy

Most businesses in Alberta fall under Alberta's own private-sector privacy law, the **Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)**. Customer data needs care, so:

- Collect only what you need.
- Never share it with advertising networks.
- Say clearly in your policy what you keep and for how long.

## Marketing email

Canada's anti-spam law, **CASL**, requires clear consent and an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

## Consumer rules

Alberta's **Internet Sales Contract Regulation** sets out what you must tell customers before they buy online. Check the current requirements with the Alberta government or a lawyer before you publish your terms of sale.
