## What it is

The **Business Number (BN)** is a nine-digit number the Canada Revenue Agency uses to identify your business. Your GST account (step 4) is attached to it.

## Separate from your trade name

Registering a trade name with an Alberta registry agent (step 2) does **not** give you a federal BN, so register with the CRA separately.

## How to get it

Register with the CRA using **Business Registration Online (BRO)**.

## Sign in with a CRA account

BRO is only available through your **CRA account**. Sign in first, then complete the registration online. If you don't have a CRA account yet, you can create one from the CRA sign-in page. You will need it later to register for GST, file returns and make payments.
