## One tax only

Alberta charges **5% GST** and has **no provincial sales tax**, so there is no second account to open as there is in BC. GST is registered, collected and filed with the **CRA**.

## When you must register

You must register once your taxable sales pass **$30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters**.

## Why register earlier

We recommend registering from the start. As a registrant you can **claim back the GST you pay** on inventory and business expenses. Ask your accountant how long you must stay registered once you opt in.

## Filing

You will choose how often to file. Annual filing is common for small businesses, but check which option applies to you. File even if you collected nothing.

## Selling outside Alberta

The rate you charge depends on where the customer receives the goods, not where you are based. A customer in Ontario pays 13% HST, for example, and some provinces have their own sales taxes. Ask your accountant before you ship outside Alberta, or begin by selling to Alberta customers only.
