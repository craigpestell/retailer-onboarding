## Lubricants

Health Canada generally regulates personal lubricants as **medical devices**. The manufacturer or importer holds the licence, and retailers are normally exempt. Ask your supplier for the documentation anyway.

## Toys

Do not repeat claims such as "body-safe" or "phthalate-free" unless your supplier can back them up in writing.

## Costumes

Textile products may need **fibre-content labelling** under Canadian rules. Confirm what applies to each item.

## Marketing

Ordinary retail products are fine. Avoid explicit imagery in advertising and on public pages.

## Check current rules

Rules change. Confirm anything here that matters to a specific product with Health Canada or the Competition Bureau.
