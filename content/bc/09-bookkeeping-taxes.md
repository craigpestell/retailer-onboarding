## Bookkeeping

Use simple software such as Wave, QuickBooks or Xero. Track sales, **GST collected**, **PST collected** and expenses separately. Keep every receipt.

## Income tax

As a sole proprietor you report business income on your personal tax return using **form T2125**.

You may need to pay income tax in **instalments** once you owe more than a few thousand dollars a year. Your accountant can tell you.

## Records

Keep your records for **six years**.

## One accountant meeting

A one-time consult early on is cheap compared with fixing mistakes later. Bring this checklist and ask about out-of-province sales.
