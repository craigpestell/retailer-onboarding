## Pages you need

- **Privacy policy**
- **Terms of sale**
- **Shipping and returns policy**

## Privacy

You fall under Canada's privacy law, **PIPEDA**. Customer data is especially sensitive in this business, so:

- Collect only what you need.
- Never share it with advertising networks.
- Say clearly in your policy what you keep and for how long.

## Age confirmation

Add an age confirmation on entry. Decide whether to use 18+ or 19+ to match BC's age of majority.

## Marketing email

Canada's anti-spam law, **CASL**, requires clear consent and an unsubscribe link in every marketing email.

## Packaging

Ship in plain packaging with a neutral return address.
