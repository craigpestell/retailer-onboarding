## What it is

BC charges **7% PST** on most goods sold to customers in BC, including online sales. It is separate from GST and is filed with a different agency.

If you sell taxable goods to BC customers, you must be registered. Registration is free.

## How to register

Register through **eTaxBC**. You will receive a **PST number**.

## Collecting and filing

You collect PST from BC customers, then file and remit on a monthly, quarterly or annual schedule. **File even if you collected nothing** for the period.

## Your supplier needs this number

Give your PST number to your supplier. It lets you buy goods **for resale without paying PST**, so you only charge PST once, to the end customer.

## Selling outside BC

BC PST applies to goods delivered in BC. Other provinces have their own rules, so ask your accountant before you ship elsewhere.
