## What to do

Apply through your city or district hall, usually online. Fees range from about $50 to a couple of hundred dollars a year.

## Home-based business

Some municipalities require a **home-occupation permit** or limit customer visits and signage. Check before you apply.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Tell them exactly what you will sell. Some municipalities have bylaws on adult-oriented retail. These often target storefronts, but you want to know before you start, not after.

## Multiple municipalities

Some areas want a licence for each municipality you operate in. Ask if that applies.
