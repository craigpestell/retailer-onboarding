## Structure

Start as a **sole proprietorship**. It is the cheapest and simplest option, and you can incorporate later if the business grows.

The trade-off is that you are **personally liable** for the business. Ontario's own guidance puts it plainly: creditors can take your personal assets and your business to pay debts. Insurance (step 6) is how you manage that risk.

## Name

- If you trade under just your own legal name (for example "Jane Smith"), you generally do not have to register a name.
- If you use any other name, for example "Jane's Gift Shop", you **must register it** in the next step.

Pick a name that is unique, easy to spell and easy to say. Search it in the Ontario Business Registry before you fall in love with it.

## Keep it practical

Choose a name you are comfortable seeing on a bank statement, a licence and a shipping label.
