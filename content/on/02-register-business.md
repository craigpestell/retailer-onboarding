## Register online

Register a **sole proprietorship** through the **Ontario Business Registry**. You need a working email address and a debit or credit card, and you create an Ontario.ca Login and Ontario Business Account along the way.

The fee is **$60**, and the registration is valid for **five years**, after which you renew it for the same fee. Registering online is immediate; by mail it takes about 15 business days.

## Who has to register

Ontario says you must register if you operate under a name other than your own legal name. Its guidance also mentions businesses with employees, facilities or offices in Ontario, so if you are unsure whether a home-based business counts, ask ServiceOntario before you skip this step.

## Your Business Identification Number

Registering gives you a nine-digit **Ontario Business Identification Number (BIN)**. It is not the same as the federal Business Number you get in the next step.

## Your address becomes public

Most information filed in the registry, including address information, goes on the **public record** and can be searched. If you work from home and do not want strangers or customers to see your address, use a **PO box or a mailing address service** instead.

## Keep it

Save the confirmation. You will need it for the bank account and your supplier account.
