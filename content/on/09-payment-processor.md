## This can block everything

Many payment processors **restrict or ban** certain product categories, and some review each business individually. Policies change, so check each one's current rules.

## What to do

1. Ask each processor **directly** whether they accept your product category.
2. Ask other retailers which processors they use and which ones dropped them.
3. Apply, and **do not build the store around any processor until you are approved**.

## Descriptor

Choose a neutral **billing descriptor**, such as "Jane's Goods", so a customer's statement is clear and easy to recognize.

## Have a backup

Approval can be withdrawn. Know your second choice before you need it.
