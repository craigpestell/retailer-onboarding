## It depends on your municipality

There is no single Ontario business licence. Each city or town decides what it licenses, so the answer can be different a few kilometres away.

- In **Toronto**, the city says retail and product stores that sell **new items and no food** do not need a municipal operating licence.
- In **Ottawa**, you may need a municipal business licence depending on your business, so check its licensing pages.

Look up your own municipality rather than assuming either answer applies to you.

## Home-based business

Even when no licence is required, **zoning bylaws** may limit what you can do from home, such as customer visits, signage, deliveries or storing stock. Check before you start.

## Be upfront about what you sell

Some products or business types carry extra bylaws or requirements, including food. Tell your municipality exactly what you will sell so you know before you start, not after.

## Keep the answer

If the city confirms you do not need a licence, get that in writing or save the page. Banks and suppliers sometimes ask.
