---
title: Open a business bank account
summary: Keep business money separate from personal money.
time: 1 hour
cost: Often a small monthly fee
checklist:
  - Bring your registration confirmation, Business Number and ID
  - Open a separate business chequing account
  - Tell the bank what you sell
  - Optionally get a business credit card
---

## Why separate

A separate account makes the books much cleaner at tax time and makes it easy to see what the business actually earns.

## What to bring

Your Ontario Business Registry confirmation, your Business Number and government ID.

## Tell the bank what you sell

Some banks and payment processors treat certain product categories as higher risk. Being upfront now avoids having an account closed later.
