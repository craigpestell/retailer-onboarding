## Regulated products

Some categories have their own rules in Canada, for example **cosmetics and personal care**, **food**, **children's products** and **electrical items**. In many cases the manufacturer or importer holds the licence or notification, and retailers are normally exempt. Ask your supplier for the documentation anyway.

## Product claims

Do not repeat claims such as "non-toxic", "organic" or "BPA-free" unless your supplier can back them up in writing.

## Clothing and textiles

Textile products may need **fibre-content labelling** under Canadian rules. Confirm what applies to each item.

## Marketing

Keep descriptions and advertising accurate and not misleading. The Competition Bureau enforces this for online sellers too.

## Check current rules

Rules change. Confirm anything here that matters to a specific product with Health Canada or the Competition Bureau.
