## One tax, not two

Ontario charges **13% HST**, which combines the federal and provincial portions into a single tax. It is registered, collected and filed with the **CRA**. Unlike BC, Ontario has **no separate provincial sales tax account** to register for.

## When you must register

You must register once your taxable sales pass **$30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters**.

## Why register earlier

We recommend registering from the start. As a registrant you can **claim back the HST you pay** on inventory and business expenses. You generally must stay registered for at least a year once you opt in.

## Filing

You will choose how often to file. Annual filing is common for small businesses, but check which option applies to you. File even if you collected nothing.

## Selling outside Ontario

The rate you charge depends on where the sale is made, which for goods you ship is **where the customer receives them**, not where you are based. The CRA's own example has a Vancouver store charging Ontario's 13% on a delivery to Toronto. Rates differ by province, and some provinces have their own taxes, so ask your accountant before you ship outside Ontario, or begin by selling to Ontario customers only.
