## Why it matters

As a sole proprietor you are personally liable. Insurance is what stands between a problem with a product and your own savings.

## What to ask for

- **General liability**
- **Product liability**, which covers claims about the products you sell
- Whether your **home insurance** covers a home-based business and its stock

## Say what you sell

**Tell every insurer exactly what you sell.** Some policies exclude certain product types, and you want to find that out before you buy a policy, not when you make a claim. Get confirmation in writing.
